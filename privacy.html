smarTrader19 Autopost: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

smarTrader19 Autopost is a private publishing tool. Its only user is the owner of the SmarTrader Facebook Page and the @smarTrader19 Instagram account, who uses it to publish their own scheduled posts to those two accounts through Meta's Graph API.

What the app accesses

  • The owner's own Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account, in order to publish posts and the first comment on those posts.
  • The IDs and links of the posts it publishes, kept so the same post is never published twice.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about anyone.
  • It does not read messages, followers, comments by other people, or any other account's data.
  • It has no sign-up and no other users.

Data deletion

The app stores no user data. To remove its access, open Facebook Settings > Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find smarTrader19 Autopost and click Remove. The post IDs it kept can be deleted on request through the SmarTrader Facebook Page.
